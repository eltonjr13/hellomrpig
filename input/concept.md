# KAZENTO Cable Vector

## Objective

Create a modular magnetic cable guide for FDM 3D printing. The product should route a cable through a low-profile base, support removable snap-fit retention, and reserve internal magnet pockets for metal-surface mounting.

## Main Function

Hold and redirect cables using a printable base, straight guide, snap cover, modular snap interface, and magnet slots.

## Required Modules

- magnetic_base_core
- straight_cable_guide
- snap_cover
- angled_redirection_module
- multi_cable_channel_module
- modular_snap_interface
- internal_magnet_slots

## Initial Dimensions

- Base length: 120 mm
- Base width: 28 mm
- Base height: 7 mm
- Guide height: 16 mm
- Cable channel width: 18 mm
- Cable channel height: 9 mm
- Corner radius: 3 mm
- Magnets: 6 mm x 2 mm
- Magnet pocket: 6.2 mm x 2.2 mm
- Magnet count: 4

## Materials

- PETG or PLA+
- 6 mm x 2 mm round magnets

## Printing Type

- FDM 3D printing

## Technical Constraints

- Minimum wall thickness: 1.6 mm
- Recommended wall thickness: 2.4 mm
- Snap-fit clearance: 0.35 mm
- Magnet housing clearance: 0.20 mm
- Avoid unsupported overhangs greater than 45 degrees
